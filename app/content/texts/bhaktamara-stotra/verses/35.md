simple_hi: 'जो स्वर्ग और मोक्ष के मार्ग का अनुसन्धान करने वालों के लिए इष्ट है, जो सद्-धर्म के तत्त्व-कथन में अद्वितीय कुशल है, जो तीनों लोकों के लिए स्पष्ट अर्थ — सब भाषाओं के स्वभाव में परिणत होने के गुण से युक्त है — आपकी ऐसी दिव्य-ध्वनि (वाणी) होती है।'

simple_en: 'Sought after by those searching for the path to heaven and to liberation; supreme in expounding the truth of *sad-dharma*; reaching the three worlds with clear meaning — naturally transforming into every language by its very quality — such is Your divine voice (*divya-dhvani*).'

translation_en: 'Sought after by those searching for the road to heaven and to liberation; supreme alone in declaring the truth of *sad-dharma*; reaching the three worlds with vivid meaning; equipped with the property of naturally transforming into every language — such is Your divine voice (*divya-dhvani*).'

meaning_en: 'Verse 35 names the eighth prātihārya — the *divya-dhvani*, the unique form of speech attributed to a Tirthankara. In Digambara tradition, the Tirthankara&apos;s voice does not arise from physical articulation; it emanates and is heard by every being in its own language simultaneously. This is one of the most distinctive teachings of Digambara doctrine. With this verse, the eight prātihāryas that began at verse 28 are complete; it is also the last of the four verses (32–35) found only in the Digambara recension.'

The eighth prātihārya completes the picture of the *samavasarana*: the tree, the throne, the fly-whisks, the umbrellas, the drum, the rain of flowers, the halo — and at the centre of it all, a voice that every being hears in its own language.

The splendor exists for the sake of the teaching.
