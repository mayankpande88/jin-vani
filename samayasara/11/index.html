Verse ११
बंधाणं च सहावं वियाणिदुं अप्पणो सहावं च।
बंधेसु जो विरज्जदि सो कम्मविमोक्खणं कुणदि॥२९३॥

baṁdhāṇaṁ ca sahāvaṁ viyāṇiduṁ appaṇo sahāvaṁ ca
baṁdhesu jo virajjadi so kamma-vimokkhaṇaṁ kuṇadi

Simple Meaning · सरल अर्थ

जो बन्धों के स्वभाव को और अपने (आत्मा के) स्वभाव को जानकर बन्धों से विरक्त होता है, वही कर्मों से मुक्ति करता है।

Knowing the nature of the bonds and the nature of the self, the one who turns away from the bonds brings about release from karma.

→ Tap any underlined Sanskrit word above to learn what it means.

Sources

  • 1.Samayasara 288–293 (Mokṣa-adhikāra) — Acharya Kundakunda — the man in chains: knowing or thinking about the bonds does not free him; cutting them does (288–292). Release comes to the one who, knowing the nature of bondage and of the self, becomes detached from bondage (293). Gatha 293 is numbered 315 in Jayasena's Tātparyavṛtti.
  • 2.Atmakhyati on Samayasara 293 — Acharya Amritchandra — the separation (dvidhākaraṇa) of the self and bondage is alone the cause of liberation.
  • 3.Samayasara 294 — Soul and bondage, cut by their own characteristics with the chisel of wisdom (prajñā), become separate.
  • 4.Tattvartha Sutra 10.2 — Mokṣa as complete release from all karma, through the absence of the causes of bondage and through nirjarā.
