Verse ९
णाणी रागप्पजहो सव्वदव्वेसु कम्ममज्झगदो।
णो लिप्पदि रजएण दु कद्दममज्झे जहा कणयं॥२१८॥

ṇāṇī rāgappajaho savva-davvesu kamma-majjhagado
ṇo lippadi rajaeṇa du kaddama-majjhe jahā kaṇayaṁ

Simple Meaning · सरल अर्थ

जैसे कीचड़ में पड़ा सोना कीचड़ से लिप्त नहीं होता, वैसे ही सब द्रव्यों के प्रति राग को छोड़ने वाला ज्ञानी, कर्मों के बीच रहते हुए भी कर्म-रज से लिप्त नहीं होता।

Just as gold lying in mud is not stained by the mud, so the knower who has given up attachment to all substances is not stained by karmic dust, even in the midst of karma.

Sources

  • 1.Samayasara 218–219 (Nirjarā-adhikāra) — Acharya Kundakunda — gold in mud is not smeared, iron in mud is; so the knower free of attachment is not smeared by karma, while the ignorant one, attached to other substances, is. Numbered 229–230 in Jayasena's Tātparyavṛtti; Vijay K. Jain's edition reads rāgappajaho hi in the first line.
  • 2.Atmakhyati on Samayasara 218–219 — Acharya Amritchandra — gold, being by nature unsmearable, is not smeared though in mud; the knower, whose nature is to give up attachment to all other substances, is likewise not smeared by karma though in its midst.
  • 3.Samayasara 193 — Opening gatha of the chapter: whatever the samyagdṛṣṭi experiences through the senses, of non-conscious and conscious things, is all a cause of nirjarā.
