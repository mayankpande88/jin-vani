Verse १०
जो सो दु णेहभावो तम्हि णरे तेण तस्स रयबंधो।
णिच्छयदो विण्णेयं ण कायचेट्ठाहिं सेसाहिं॥२४०॥

jo so du ṇeha-bhāvo tamhi ṇare teṇa tassa raya-baṁdho
ṇicchayado viṇṇeyaṁ ṇa kāya-ceṭṭhāhiṁ sesāhiṁ

Simple Meaning · सरल अर्थ

उस पुरुष में जो तेल की चिकनाई है, उसी से उसको धूल का बन्ध होता है — यह निश्चय से जानो; शरीर की बाकी चेष्टाओं से नहीं।

The oiliness on that man — that alone is why the dust sticks to him. Know this as the real cause; it is not the rest of his bodily activity.

Sources

  • 1.Samayasara 237–241 (Bandha-adhikāra) — Acharya Kundakunda — the oiled man exercising in dust: the dust binds because of the oil, not the exercise (240); likewise the person of wrong belief is bound by producing attachment in consciousness amid his activities (241). Gatha 240 is numbered 255 in Jayasena's Tātparyavṛtti.
  • 2.Atmakhyati on Samayasara 237–241 — Acharya Amritchandra — by elimination: not the world full of karmic matter, not activity, not the senses, not harm to beings; attachment and the like in consciousness alone is the cause of bondage.
  • 3.Tattvartha Sutra 6.4 — The activity of one with passion brings sāmparāyika influx; of one without passion, īryāpatha influx.
  • 4.Tattvartha Sutra 8.2 — Bondage is the soul, being with passion (sakaṣāya), taking in matter fit to become karma.
