Verse ३३
नैगमसंग्रहव्यवहारर्जुसूत्रशब्दसमभिरूढैवंभूता नयाः॥३३॥

naigama-saṁgraha-vyavahārarjusūtra-śabda-samabhirūḍhaivaṁbhūtā nayāḥ

Simple Meaning · सरल अर्थ

नैगम, संग्रह, व्यवहार, ऋजुसूत्र, शब्द, समभिरूढ और एवंभूत — ये सात नय हैं।

The seven *nayas* (standpoints) are: naigama, saṁgraha, vyavahāra, ṛjusūtra, śabda, samabhirūḍha and evaṁbhūta.

Sources

  • 1.Sarvarthasiddhi 1.33 — Pujyapada — the general definition of naya and its two kinds (dravyārthika, paryāyārthika); the mark of each of the seven, with examples; the first four as artha-naya, the last three as śabda-naya; a naya held in isolation is mithyā, held in relation to the others it is samyak.
